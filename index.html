CensorUp - Local

A Video & Audio Censor Tool

CensorUp - local is a custom fork of the original CensorUp project.

--- Why This Exists ---

The original application had a few significant complexities and downsides. Chief among them was being INCREDIBLY complicated to configure, requiring a massive stack of dependencies and deep technical setup knowledge (even when following the setup guide carefully).

--- What's Different? ---

With CensorUp-Local, the entire infrastructure is now ENTIRELY bundled neatly into a single, executable desktop file!

No external cloud storage dependencies, no confusing API configs. Everything is processed 100% locally on your device.
